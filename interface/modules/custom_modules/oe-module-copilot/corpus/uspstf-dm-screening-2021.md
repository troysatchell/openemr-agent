#### Recommendation (Grade B)

The USPSTF recommends **screening for prediabetes and type 2 diabetes in adults
aged 35 to 70 years who have overweight or obesity**. Clinicians should offer or
refer patients with prediabetes to effective preventive interventions. This is a
**Grade B** recommendation.

<!-- chunk: uspstf-dm.interval | source: uspstf-dm-screening-2021 | derived_from: USPSTF 2021 §Screening Interval -->
#### Screening interval

Screening every **3 years** may be a reasonable interval for adults with normal
blood glucose results, though the optimal interval is uncertain.

<!-- chunk: uspstf-dm.higher-risk | source: uspstf-dm-screening-2021 | derived_from: USPSTF 2021 §Risk Assessment -->
#### Earlier or expanded screening

Consider screening at a **younger age or a lower BMI** in persons at higher risk
— including those with a family history of diabetes, a history of gestational
diabetes or polycystic ovary syndrome, or membership in a population with higher
prevalence (including American Indian/Alaska Native, Asian American, Black,
Hispanic/Latino, or Native Hawaiian/Pacific Islander persons).
