<!-- chunk: t2dm.diagnosis | source: protocol-t2dm-v1 | derived_from: ADA 2026 §Diagnosis and Classification -->
#### Diagnostic criteria

Diagnose diabetes by any of: **A1c ≥6.5%**; **fasting plasma glucose ≥126
mg/dL** (fasting ≥8 h); **2-hour plasma glucose ≥200 mg/dL** during a 75-g oral
glucose tolerance test; or a **random plasma glucose ≥200 mg/dL** in a patient
with classic hyperglycemia symptoms. In the absence of unequivocal
hyperglycemia, confirm with a repeat test (same test repeated, or two different
tests both above threshold). Prediabetes: A1c 5.7–6.4%, FPG 100–125 mg/dL, or
2-h OGTT 140–199 mg/dL.

<!-- chunk: t2dm.screening | source: protocol-t2dm-v1 | derived_from: USPSTF Prediabetes and Type 2 Diabetes Screening -->
#### Screening (asymptomatic adults)

Screen adults aged **35 to 70 years** who have **overweight or obesity** for
prediabetes and type 2 diabetes (USPSTF B recommendation); repeat at least every
three years if normal. Offer or refer patients with prediabetes to effective
preventive interventions. Screen earlier or at a lower BMI threshold in
higher-risk groups (family history, high-risk race/ethnicity, history of
gestational diabetes).

<!-- chunk: t2dm.glycemic-target | source: protocol-t2dm-v1 | derived_from: ADA 2026 §Glycemic Targets -->
#### Glycemic targets

Individualize the A1c goal. **<7%** is a reasonable target for many non-pregnant
adults without significant hypoglycemia. Choose a **less stringent** goal (e.g.,
<8%) for patients with limited life expectancy, extensive comorbidity,
hypoglycemia unawareness, or where the harms of tight control outweigh benefit;
a **more stringent** goal is appropriate if achievable without hypoglycemia.
Reassess targets as circumstances change.

<!-- chunk: t2dm.pharma-first-line | source: protocol-t2dm-v1 | derived_from: ADA 2026 §Pharmacologic Therapy -->
#### Pharmacologic therapy — comorbidity-driven selection

Choose glucose-lowering therapy by the patient's comorbidities, not by A1c
alone. **Metformin** remains a foundational agent for many. Independent of A1c,
prefer agents with organ-protective benefit where the indication exists: a
**GLP-1 receptor agonist** or **SGLT2 inhibitor** with proven cardiovascular
benefit in patients with, or at high risk for, atherosclerotic cardiovascular
disease; an **SGLT2 inhibitor** for heart failure or chronic kidney disease. A
**GLP-1-based therapy is recommended as initial pharmacotherapy** for adults
with type 2 diabetes and liver fibrosis or MASLD. Layer therapy to reach the
individualized target; reassess at 3-month intervals.

<!-- chunk: t2dm.technology | source: protocol-t2dm-v1 | derived_from: ADA 2026 §Diabetes Technology -->
#### Diabetes technology

Continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) and automated insulin delivery are
recommended **earlier and with fewer restrictions** in the 2026 Standards,
including soon after diagnosis for appropriate patients on insulin or at risk of
hypoglycemia. Use CGM data (time-in-range, hypoglycemia exposure) alongside A1c
to guide titration.
