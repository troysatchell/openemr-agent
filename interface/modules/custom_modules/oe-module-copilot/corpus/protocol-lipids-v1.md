# `protocol-lipids-v1` · Lipid Management — Primary Prevention Protocol

- **source_type:** practice_protocol · **license:** authored (repo license)

- **last_reviewed:** 2026-07-13
- **derived_from:** USPSTF, Statin Use for the Primary Prevention of Cardiovascular Disease in Adults (2022); AHA/ACC 2025 Hypertension Guideline §Risk Assessment (PREVENT) — for risk-tool alignment
- **scope:** statin therapy in primary prevention in adults without known CVD. Secondary prevention and familial hypercholesterolemia out of scope.

<!-- chunk: lipids.statin-primary-high | source: protocol-lipids-v1 | derived_from: USPSTF 2022 Statin Primary Prevention (Grade B) -->
#### Statin therapy — higher-risk adults (offer)

Prescribe a **statin** for adults aged **40–75 years** who have **one or more
CVD risk factors** (dyslipidemia, diabetes, hypertension, or smoking) **and** an
estimated **10-year CVD risk ≥10%**, after discussing the rationale and with the
patient's agreement (USPSTF **Grade B** — moderate net benefit). A
moderate-intensity statin is the usual starting choice for primary prevention.

<!-- chunk: lipids.statin-primary-intermediate | source: protocol-lipids-v1 | derived_from: USPSTF 2022 Statin Primary Prevention (Grade C) -->
#### Statin therapy — intermediate-risk adults (selectively offer)

For adults aged **40–75 years** with **one or more CVD risk factors** and an
estimated **10-year CVD risk of 7.5% to <10%**, the net benefit is smaller.
**Selectively offer** a statin, individualizing on patient values and
preferences (USPSTF **Grade C** — the decision should weigh a small potential
benefit against the burden of daily medication).

<!-- chunk: lipids.statin-elderly | source: protocol-lipids-v1 | derived_from: USPSTF 2022 Statin Primary Prevention (I statement) -->
#### Adults 76 and older

Evidence is **insufficient** to assess the balance of benefits and harms of
initiating a statin for primary prevention in adults **aged 76 years or older**
(USPSTF **I statement**). Individualize based on overall health, competing
risks, life expectancy, and patient preference.

<!-- chunk: lipids.risk-tools | source: protocol-lipids-v1 | derived_from: USPSTF 2022; AHA/ACC 2025 (PREVENT) -->
#### Estimating 10-year cardiovascular risk

Estimate risk with a validated CVD risk calculator before starting therapy. Note
a tool difference the co-pilot should surface if relevant: USPSTF eligibility
was defined using pooled-cohort-equation risk, whereas the AHA/ACC 2025
guideline now uses the **PREVENT** equations, which are better calibrated and
generally estimate lower absolute risk than the pooled cohort equations for the
same patient. When risk sits near a decision threshold, note which tool produced
the estimate.
